FLOW
1. Student na Approved lang ang pwedeng mag-apply (max 3 pending) o mag-request.
2. Request ng bagong company/department -> pending; lalabas sa coordinator Host Training > Requests (kita ang student at company),
   approve/decline doon (existing). May notification ang coordinator (bell) at ang student (approve/decline).
3. Bago i-send: habang nagta-type, may warning kung may kapareho nang company/department (kasama ang pending).
   - Exact na department sa company = hindi pwedeng ipadala.
   - Kapareho ang company = kailangan piliin ang existing ("It's this company") o i-tick na "different company".
4. Apply sa department -> coordinator bell + Students > Company applications tab.
   Accept & deploy -> student.department = napili, status = Deployed, deployed date = ngayon,
   ibang pending apps niya = cancelled, may notification ang student. Decline -> kailangan ng reason (private, kita ng student).
5. Deployed (o Completed) lang ang may Weekly Report, DTR, Post-Deployment (frontend at backend).
